2 · Candidate decisions and lineage

3 · Methodology, provenance and limits
No matched, separately trained no-exchange full score field is available in this export. The page makes no feature-exchange Top-K coverage improvement claim. V/fusion/B2 proposals use the same bank and NMS settings; GEOQ selection diagnostics use exactly its existing Support20 bank. Different proposal banks are not presented as an identical-candidate ranker ablation.
Frozen fullTrain12 B2 checkpoints (seeds 42/43); these New12 scenes are excluded from B2 training but their visual teacher and later research have seen them. Scores are replayed from cached inputs; B2 global Top1, all 20 Support candidates/centers and GEOQ selections are checked against historical artifacts. Exact candidate scores and decisions are stored separately from quantized display fields. Small score gaps may be below display precision.
Visual raw score = negative scaled-distance sum divided by 40 (historical implementation); acoustic raw = FP-RIR embedding cosine; fixed fusion = 0.85 × standardized visual + 0.15 × standardized acoustic. B2 uses its adapted visual/acoustic representations with the same fusion weights. “Local support” is a score-mass heuristic, not calibrated pose probability. GT-neighborhood comparisons below each heatmap use the full bank, regardless of the displayed slice.
Only selected derived score grids, candidate summaries, low-resolution RGB thumbnails and floorplan maps are published. No RIR recordings, embeddings, checkpoints or complete RGB dataset. No new sensor-noise/environment-change evaluation is implied.
