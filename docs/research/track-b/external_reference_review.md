## Additional relevant primary sources discovered

- [DisCo-FLoc](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01822), January2026 preprint: ray-regression candidates followed by position/orientation contrastive visual-geometric disambiguation without extra semantic labels. Candidate-conditioned geometry discrimination itself is therefore not a novel contribution claim. Our required new discriminator must be distinguished by independent RGB/RIR evidence, actual OOF full-field proposals and demonstrated incremental value, not by its name.
- [From Uncertainty to Determinism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26817), July2026, revised August2026: image-conditioned pose diffusion proposes modes and a localized refiner predicts bounded pose corrections from floorplan crops. This is a relevant alternative to exhaustive ray matching, but replacing our full-bank scorer with its sampled candidates would change the user's required main protocol. Keep it an external comparison/alternative, with candidate coverage explicitly separate.
- [PadLoc](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05172), submitted November3,2026: a training-free geometric backend with sparse wall-orientation candidates and temporal mixture filtering, designed around a swappable depth front end. Its abstract reports comparisons while holding competitor depth fixed. This motivates equal-input backend controls and cautions against claiming that lack of per-map training or dense banks alone is novel. It is a recent preprint; code/evaluation scope has not been independently audited here.

## Research decision and remaining work

Retain exact single-observation, full-bank RGB+observed6chRIR+known2D FP as Track B's required experimental boundary. The defensible contribution would need evidence that acoustic structural information and candidate-conditioned cross-modal interaction improve localization/reliability beyond strong independent fusion, with capacity controls, no candidate RIR references, a genuinely impairing visual shift and complete deployment cost. Existing negative interaction results and severe-occlusion negative transfer mean that this claim is currently unproven.

Our use of ray geometry, foundation depth features, uncertainty calibration, Top20 selection or a geometry discriminator is not by itself novel. The external summaries do not prove absence of other RGB/RIR work; a broader audio-visual localization and echo-matching novelty search and full-paper examination remain necessary before publication claims. Review/reproduction priorities are observation-only F3Loc/UnLoc under equal inputs and clear exposure limits, semantic-input feasibility for SemRayLoc/Ye, then equal-input backend alternatives. No automatic dataset acquisition or external upload was performed for this review.
