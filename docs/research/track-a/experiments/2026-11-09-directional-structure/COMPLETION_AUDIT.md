## Scientific stopping decision

The requested useful method has been found and checked on scene-held New12 and a frozen exploratory Transfer11: preserve visual candidate coordinates and rerank by acoustic-to-FP coarse structure. Competing scene-prior, channel-identity, model-capacity, target-ceiling and integration explanations have explicit controls. Negative cosine/GEOQ comparisons and fullbank fine degradation delimit its value. Unused-scene/real-sensor validation would support a stronger future claim, but was not required to call this exploratory mechanism result useful. No further fit is needed for the present question. Administrative completion requires Docs validation and successful normal push.
